Link Your Discord

Before you can continue, we need your Discord User ID to connect your account.

How to find your Discord User ID:

  1. 1 Open Discord and click the ⚙️ Settings icon at the bottom left (next to your profile picture)
  2. 2 Scroll down in the left sidebar and click on Advanced
  3. 3 Toggle on Developer Mode
  4. 4 Close Settings and go back to Discord
  5. 5 Click on your profile icon at the bottom left
  6. 6 Click Copy User ID
  7. 7 Paste the ID into the field above and click Continue

Your Discord ID will be permanently linked to your account. Make sure you enter the correct ID.
